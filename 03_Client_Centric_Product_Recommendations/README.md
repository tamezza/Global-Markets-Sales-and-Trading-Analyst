# Part 3 — Client-Centric Product Recommendations

**Status:** ✅ Done

## Task brief

A new client, **Summit Investments** (Equities 45% · Fixed Income 30% · REITs 15% · Cash 10%), wants to become more diversified and more sustainable while growing at a moderate pace. The task has three steps:

1. Rank the client's priorities. The correct order is diversification, risk mitigation, sustainability, moderate growth, long-term stability.
2. Match each priority to a financial product.
3. Write a **Strategic Recommendation Proposal** using Bank of America's template.

## My approach

1. Read the Client Profile and the Product Alignment Guide. Matched each priority to a product: emerging-market bonds, corporate and Treasury bonds, green ETFs, an ESG mutual fund and REITs.
2. Built a proposed allocation in `analysis.ipynb` and checked it against the client's targets: at least 10% in green ETFs and ESG funds, at most 8% in emerging-market bonds, and less reliance on U.S. large-caps.
3. Measured expected return, volatility, risk contribution and concentration under clearly stated, illustrative capital-market assumptions.
4. Stress-tested both portfolios against the client's three concerns: rising rates, an equity sell-off and an emerging-market crisis.
5. Wrote the proposal in LaTeX, following the template's sections, with every figure and table generated by the notebook.

## Key takeaways

- **Proposed mix:** U.S. large-cap 27 / international ETF 5 / green ETFs 7 / ESG mutual fund 6 / core bonds 22 (shorter duration) / green bonds 4 / emerging-market bonds 6 / REITs 13 / cash 10.
- **Concentration was the hidden problem:** U.S. large-caps were 45% of the capital but **73% of the risk**. After the change they are 43% of the risk, and the effective number of holdings goes from 3.1 to 6.1.
- **Diversifying need not add risk:** expected return goes from 5.8% to 5.9% while volatility falls from 9.4% to 9.2%. A first draft was *riskier* than the current portfolio, so I rebalanced it before writing the proposal.
- **Every choice has a cost, so show it:** emerging-market bonds make the portfolio lose more in an emerging-market crisis (−2.2% vs −1.4%). The proposal caps them, phases them in and sets a trigger to reduce them.
- **Read the whole client profile:** sustainability is objective #1 on the sheet, yet it ranks 3rd in Forage's answer.
