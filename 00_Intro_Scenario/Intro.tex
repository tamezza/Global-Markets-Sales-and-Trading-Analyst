Welcome to your first step in mastering the world of sales and trading. This
page is designed to provide you with a solid foundation of essential terms,
concepts, and tools that will guide you throughout the simulation.

\heading{Key Terms to Know}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Risk-Adjusted Returns}: A measurement of the return on an
  investment relative to the risk taken to achieve it. This helps evaluate the
  efficiency of a trading strategy.
  \item \textbf{Equity Derivatives}: Financial instruments whose value is
  derived from equity securities, such as options or futures contracts.
  \item \textbf{Trading Algorithms}: Pre-programmed strategies that execute
  trades automatically based on defined market conditions.
  \item \textbf{Market Volatility}: The rate at which the price of a security
  or market index changes over time, reflecting uncertainty or risk.
  \item \textbf{Liquidity}: The ease with which an asset can be bought or sold
  in the market without affecting its price.
\end{itemize}

\heading{Essential Insights}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Understanding Market Trends}: Financial markets are influenced
  by global events, policy changes, and economic indicators like inflation,
  interest rates, and employment data. Recognizing these trends is key to
  making informed decisions.
  \item \textbf{Balancing Risk and Reward}: Effective strategies minimize risks
  while maximizing returns. Always consider the potential downside of a
  decision before committing to an action.
  \item \textbf{Client-Centric Thinking}: Successful sales and trading analysts
  prioritize their client's objectives, tailoring solutions to align with
  their unique goals and constraints.
\end{itemize}

\heading{Innovative Tips}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
