# Part 1 — Market Overview and Insight Generation

**Status:** ✅ Done

## Task brief

Analyse seven weeks of market data (interest rates, sector returns, VIX, sentiment, economic events) for a hypothetical client, **Emerald Investments** (Technology 50% · Healthcare 30% · Cash 20%, moderate risk, 5–10 year horizon), and write a Market Insight Report with key trends, portfolio implications and strategic recommendations.

## My approach

1. Loaded and cleaned the weekly dataset in Python (`analysis.ipynb`).
2. Computed per-sector return, volatility, compounded return, worst week, return per unit of risk and correlations with the VIX and interest rates.
3. Measured the client portfolio's risk decomposition — technology holds 50% of the capital but drives **~91% of the risk**.
4. Proposed a new allocation and tested it on the same period against the current one.
5. Wrote the report in LaTeX, with all figures and tables generated by the notebook.

## Key takeaways

- **Concentration, not just allocation, is the risk:** a 50% weight produced ~91% of portfolio risk.
- **Proposed mix** — Tech 40 / Healthcare 30 / Financials 10 / Energy 5 / Cash 15: slightly higher return (2.24% vs 2.06%), **14% lower volatility**, **32% smaller max drawdown** on the same data.
- Energy tracked interest rates (correlation 0.96), not geopolitical events — a useful hedge against the portfolio's rate sensitivity.
- Seven weeks is a short sample: results show direction, not a forecast.
